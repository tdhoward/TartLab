Update and machine hardware results
-----------------------------------
The fixture initially booted healthy IDE on modern-v0.16.0, with zero
failures and no update marker. All 12 signed candidate update assets were
authenticated and staged over USB. Applying modern-v0.16.1 through the
installed updater left pending_health and retained the old committed

zero failures and no update marker. Selected-app, /device and /files/user
digests matched before and after. This exercises the installed v0.16.0
updater with a locally staged candidate; it does not claim internet delivery.

A device probe reported ST77922 display, ST77922Touch input, direct RGB565,
frame synchronization and exclusive display ownership. Fifty UI/direct
ownership cycles returned to UI ownership with no pending transfer. Five
Wi-Fi scans ran alongside 150 display frames without scan errors; minimum
free heap during that workload was 7,601,136 bytes and filesystem free space
was 10,158,080 of 12,517,376 bytes. All 25 initialization/deinitialization
cycles completed and returned to UI ownership. A ROM identification read
confirmed ESP32-S3 revision v0.2 and reset the board into normal startup.

Tim reported all three candidate observations passed: three power cycles
reached a readable, correctly oriented launcher/IDE; touch worked across
center, edges and corners along with chooser, Settings, Wi-Fi, brightness,
Back/Cancel and app exit; and the browser IDE could create, edit, save,

working recovery Wi-Fi, Retry normal boot, Force IDE on next boot,

credentials or user-file contents.
