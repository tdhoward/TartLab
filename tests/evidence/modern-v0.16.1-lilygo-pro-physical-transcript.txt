Normal OTA and candidate observations
-------------------------------------
The fixture initially ran modern-v0.16.0. All 12 signed candidate update
assets were authenticated and staged over USB. Applying modern-v0.16.1 via
the installed updater left pending_health and retained the committed old
version until first healthy boot. That boot reported modern-v0.16.1, zero
failures, and no marker. Selected-app, /device and /files/user digests were
identical before and after this OTA. This exercises the installed v0.16.0
updater with a locally staged candidate; it does not claim internet delivery.

Tim reported all three short candidate checks passed: three power cycles
reached a readable, correctly oriented launcher/IDE; touch and navigation
worked across center, edges, corners, chooser, Settings, Wi-Fi, brightness,
Back/Cancel and app exit; and the browser IDE could create, edit, save,
reopen, run and delete a small file and show update controls without
installing an update.

The device probe reported ST7796, CST226 touch, direct RGB565 and exclusive
display ownership. Fifty UI/direct ownership cycles ended in UI ownership
with no pending transfer. Five Wi-Fi scans ran alongside 150 display frames
without transfer or scan failure, with 10,387,456 of 12,517,376 filesystem
bytes free at that point. Twenty-five initialization/deinitialization cycles
all completed and returned to UI ownership. The probe reported 16 MiB
physical flash and the pinned MicroPython 1.27.0 build.

Recovery and protected state
----------------------------
A one-bit-corrupted staged assetfiles.tar was rejected before installation;
the version remained modern-v0.16.1 and no update marker appeared. During
a recovery-install test, the helper emitted its serial power-loss signal
before assetfiles.tar extraction and detected actual USB removal. Tim
reconnected after five seconds and confirmed the expected blank LCD and
working recovery Wi-Fi. He confirmed Retry normal boot, Force IDE on next
boot, Install latest corrective release, and Redacted diagnostic status were
available, with no credentials or user-file contents exposed. He did not
explicitly confirm seeing the white LCD signal, so that visual observation
is not claimed.

The device resumed all staged authenticated packages offline. A subsequent
uninterrupted retry boot reported healthy IDE, zero failures, committed
modern-v0.16.1 and no update marker. An early follow-up console probe had
interrupted a preceding boot before health completion; that attempt was not
counted as a pass. The successful retry waited 70 seconds before inspection.
User-file digest after recovery differs from the pre-OTA digest, but the
operator had edited user files in the intervening browser test. There is no
pre-recovery digest after those edits, so this run cannot independently
attribute that difference or assert recovery preserved exact user-file bytes.
The earlier normal OTA did preserve its before/after protected digests.

Clean provisioning and final state
----------------------------------
The authenticated clean provisioner erased and installed the same signed
candidate, with a live host subprocess deliberately terminated at final
boot-file activation. Its receipt records completed_before_interrupt=false;
it does not prove the subprocess had already written specific bytes. An
authenticated resume completed the journal after a healthy boot. The
installed managed inventory matched all 81 files in the signed prepared
image, with none missing, extra or changed. After the serial inventory probe,
Tim saw only the TartLab version on the IDE screen. He pressed RESET with
touch and buttons released, waited for boot, and confirmed the full IDE,
settings control and touch returned. The partial display immediately after
the raw serial probe is not counted as a normal boot observation.

The full modern-v0.16.0 Pro physical transcript remains applicable context
for the unchanged firmware and recovery implementation. This candidate's
fresh checks above cover the changed shared runtime, browser, update and
host provisioning path without claiming the older power-loss campaign was
rerun in full.
